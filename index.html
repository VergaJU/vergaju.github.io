About me

I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Galway developing AI-driven methods for biomedical research and clinical data analysis. My work combines machine learning, agentic AI, computational biology, and software engineering to build intelligent systems that accelerate scientific discovery. My research spans cancer genomics, clinical prediction, single-cell and spatial omics, and evolutionary optimization. I'm particularly interested in bringing these technologies into industry, where AI can transform biomedical research and healthcare.
