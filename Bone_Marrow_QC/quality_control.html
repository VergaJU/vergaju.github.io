Bone Marrow scRNA-Seq Quality Control

Comprehensive single-cell RNA sequencing quality control reports from my PhD research on the bone marrow microenvironment in multiple myeloma.

About the Project

This project involved the systematic quality control of single-cell RNA-seq data from 500+ samples spanning multiple public datasets (PRJNA511584, PRJNA579391, PRJNA492227, PRJNA694128, PRJNA723584, PRJNA732205). Samples include Multiple Myeloma (MM), Smoldering Myeloma (SMM), Monoclonal Gammopathy of Undetermined Significance (MGUS), and healthy donors.

Each sample was processed through a standardised pre-processing pipeline including alignment, barcode filtering, UMI deduplication, and cell-level QC metric computation. The reports provide per-sample visual summaries of gene detection, mitochondrial content, ribosomal content, and cell complexity to inform downstream filtering decisions.
